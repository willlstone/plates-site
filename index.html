Train

Every set, worked out before you walk in.

Pick your starting numbers once. Plates builds percentage-based cycles on a training max, with warm-ups, a heavy top set for as many reps as you can, and supplemental volume.

  • Every weight rounded to the plates you own, with plates per side on each set
  • Rest timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Log a full set in one tap, or exactly the reps you got when one falls short
  • Estimated 1RM and personal records worked out automatically
  • Training maxes go up each cycle when you hit your reps, and hold when you don't
  • Assistance work balanced across push, pull and core, or build your own program

Fuel

Macros that know it's squat day.

Plates sets daily calorie and macro targets from your stats and goal, and adds carbs on the days you lift. Rest days drop back automatically.

  • Search 12,000+ foods from the USDA
  • Scan a barcode, or read a nutrition label with the camera
  • Snap a photo of your meal and get an estimate on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence (supported iPhones)
  • Save meals you eat often and log them in one tap
  • Portions in grams, ounces, cups or servings

Consistency

Streaks that count weeks, not days.

A week counts when you finish your planned workouts, so rest days never break your streak. Plates is built to keep you coming back, not to make you feel bad.

  • Shields cover a missed week; pause for illness or travel
  • A five-week view of workouts, PRs and food logged
  • Milestones like the 1,000 lb club
  • Optional reminders that encourage, never nag

Setup

Six quick steps, then you lift.

Tell Plates your lifts, your lifting days and your goal. It works out training maxes, daily targets and a full program. Want to look around first? Try the sample data.
